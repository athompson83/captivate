# Product Definition

## Mission

Make it dramatically easier to create presentations that feel like designed digital experiences rather than static slide decks.

## Primary users

- Educators and instructors building lecture experiences.
- Trainers delivering repeatable internal or customer education.
- Executives presenting strategy, performance, and proposals.
- Creators who need polished visual storytelling without motion-design expertise.

## Jobs to be done

1. Turn a topic, outline, source material, or existing presentation into a compelling presentation journey.
2. Author or revise individual moments without regenerating the entire experience.
3. Keep presentation content, lecture notes, speaker notes, and media together.
4. Present confidently with private controls and audience-safe output.
5. Record presentation + camera/audio and produce a shareable video.
6. Reuse themes, layouts, scenes, assets, notes, and presentation structures.

## Differentiation

Captivate should win through the combination of:

- web-native spatial/animated storytelling;
- excellent authoring UX;
- deep presenter tooling;
- first-class lecture/speaker notes;
- structured AI assistance;
- recording and export;
- modern collaboration and reuse.

AI alone is not the moat. The product system, content model, presenter reliability, reusable design language, and accumulated user assets/workflows matter more.

## Product constraints

Captivate must remain useful without AI. Presentations must remain editable and renderable even when an AI provider is unavailable.
